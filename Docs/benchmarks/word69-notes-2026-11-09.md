median across the two Word formats and CPU domains.

## Method and source

The candidate Word and common Core binaries come from
`d69bcfb78b4fdd9b22121e9ea82e7dd9165fa2c8`. All readers use identical Core,
workload and auxiliary binaries. The workload and auxiliary binaries carry
forward from the November 8 comparison. The data companion records the exact
hashes and separate source identities. The original note-preserving reader
is defined in the [reconstruction packet](references/legacy-word-notes-baseline/README.md).
Before timing, the current runtimes passed all 36 candidate and reference
output checks.

PowerForge 3.0.189 ran the same fixed 16-launch comparison on the two
Windows L3 domains. Each group used reference, candidate, candidate, reference
order, three warmups and 15 measured iterations per launch. The host-load
admission samples were 8%, 26% and 30%. Start and end snapshots across
individual launches ranged from 22% to 97%. Every measurement is retained.
Those snapshots do not identify the cause of an individual slow operation.

The budgets are 5% for mean managed allocation and 10% each for median and
P95 elapsed time. There are 30 observations per reader, workload and domain.
Each invocation validates ordered content and diagnostics, and saved output
also undergoes schema validation and reopen. The measurements describe these
synthetic workloads under the recorded conditions.
