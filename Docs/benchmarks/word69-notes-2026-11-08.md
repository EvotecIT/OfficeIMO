## Launch variation and limits

The failures do not share one early-call pattern. In the second domain-B control
launch, small Save is slowest at measured iteration 13 (165.16 ms), while large
Inspect is slowest at iteration 4 (1,459.73 ms). The next candidate control
launch has its largest large-Inspect observation at iteration 0 (1,862.24 ms).
For domain-A Word95Notes Medium Save, the candidate launch maxima are 53.51 ms
and 18.91 ms; the reference launch maxima are 21.73 ms and 28.83 ms. All those
observations remain in the pooled statistics. The raw data and per-launch
summaries retain the full variation rather than presenting one launch as stable.

Start/end CPU snapshots describe ordinary workstation activity; they do not
attribute a particular delay to host load. Earlier traces established transient
runtime phases but did not prove a complete cause for these current losses.
No exclusive-host condition is claimed. Managed allocation includes the
invocation boundary; working-set delta is end-minus-start, not retained or peak
memory. This run does not establish portable absolute timing or universal
producer compatibility.

Earlier fixed source cohorts failed 5 groups/15 metric cells (`3bcb`), 2/5
(`a0d`), 3/7 (`495`) and 3/6 (`8221`). The current `2ff` cohort fails 2/3.
These are separate captures, not a controlled comparison of absolute timings or
failure counts. The companion preserves every earlier failed budget value.

## Contract and method

The [workload contract](../../OfficeIMO.Migration.Benchmarks/README.md) defines
Word69 at 100/1,000/10,000 alternating bold runs and the Word69Notes/FIB101 and
Word95Notes/FIB104 profiles at 10/100/1,000 alternating automatic footnotes and
endnotes, with two paragraphs per note. LoadInspect times public load plus
inspection; ConvertSave times public load and DOCX serialization into memory.
Generation, expected results and complete output validation remain outside timing.

Ordinary-document validation checks every ordered run, text and bold flag,
source format and loss report; saved output is schema-validated and reopened.
Its empty SemanticDigest is not used as equality evidence. Note validation
checks every body reference and position, both note kinds, complete ordered
paragraph text, numbering and placement, note parts and reference identities,
saved schema and public reopen. A reader that omits notes is not an equivalent
performance reference.

The fixed matrix uses 16 fresh processes across two pairs and two CPU domains.
Each pair and domain uses four launches in reference/candidate/candidate/reference
order, with rotated case ordering, three warmups, 15 measured
iterations per launch, Normal priority and BeforeIteration cleanup. Warmups skip
measured-result validation and metrics; that schedule is identical for both
readers. No outliers are removed. The 720 control and 1,440 note observations
provide 30 samples per reader/cell/domain. PowerForge 3.0.189 owns summaries,
comparisons and gates; publication selects their results without changing them.

The Windows 11 Pro build 26300 workstation has an AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D2,
16 physical/32 logical processors, firmware 3702 and the High performance plan.
Native L3 domains use separate masks `0xFFFF` and `0xFFFF0000`. Measurement uses
PowerShell 7.6.9 on .NET 10.0.14; builds use SDK 10.0.403. Runtime defaults,
including tiered compilation, are unchanged.
