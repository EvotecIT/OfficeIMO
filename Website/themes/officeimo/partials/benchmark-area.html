How to read these results

Full runs are complete BenchmarkDotNet measurements. Quick runs are shorter diagnostic runs; they are shown so every contender and platform is visible, and carry less weight. "1.6× faster" is the geometric mean of the per-case time ratios, so one large case doesn't dominate. Each run links to its raw JSON with every sample, the source commit and the recorded environment. Measure on hardware close to yours before deciding.
