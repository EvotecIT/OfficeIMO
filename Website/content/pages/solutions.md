OfficeIMO is a set of focused document engines and adapters rather than one closed conversion box. That shape works well when an application needs to combine creation, editing, conversion, extraction, validation, and automation while retaining control of storage, authentication, retention, and deployment.

## Choose by outcome

### [Modernize legacy Office files](/solutions/legacy-office-modernization/)

Inventory and migrate DOC, XLS, XLSB, PPT, and related templates or slide shows into modern formats without pretending every feature converts identically.

### [Publish documents](/solutions/document-publishing/)

Create Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, HTML, Markdown, RTF, CSV, and OpenDocument output from application data, then deliver it through your own service or pipeline.

### [Ingest documents for search and AI](/solutions/document-ingestion/)

Normalize text, tables, metadata, images, and chunks across document families while keeping extraction behavior inspectable.

### [Automate documents with PowerShell](/solutions/powershell-document-automation/)

Use first-party cmdlets and task-oriented aliases for reports, administration scripts, scheduled jobs, and CI/CD.

### [Bridge Office and Google Workspace](/solutions/google-workspace-migration/)

Translate Word, Excel, and PowerPoint content to and from Docs, Sheets, and Slides with explicit Drive, authentication, synchronization, and fidelity boundaries.

## What remains in your control

OfficeIMO runs as libraries and tools in your application boundary. Files do not need to be uploaded to a third-party conversion service. You choose where documents are stored, which credentials are used, how findings are logged, and whether a conversion should continue.

Start with [format support](/compatibility/) when file fidelity is the first concern, the [conversion routes](/convert/) when input and output are already known, or the [package catalog](/downloads/) when you are ready to assemble a deployment.
