Iron Software offers commercial .NET products including IronPDF, IronXL, IronWord, and IronPPT, individually and through Iron Suite. OfficeIMO offers focused MIT-licensed packages for those document families plus email and mailbox stores, native OneNote, OpenDocument, text formats, normalized extraction, and PowerShell.

Iron Software facts were last checked on 27 July 2026 against its official [IronPDF licensing page](https://ironpdf.com/licensing/) and [IronWord API overview](https://ironsoftware.com/csharp/word/object-reference/api/). Licensing dimensions, redistribution rights, air-gapped use, support, products, and prices can change; verify the controlling agreement for the actual delivery model.

## Compare the product and license boundary

| Question | OfficeIMO | Iron Software |
| --- | --- | --- |
| License | MIT | Proprietary trial, product, suite, subscription, and enterprise options |
| Source access | Public source | Commercial binaries |
| Main Office and PDF families | Focused Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDF packages | IronWord, IronXL, IronPPT, IronPDF, and adjacent suite products |
| Email, PST/OST, native OneNote, Reader | First-party packages and adapters | Verify the required product or build the integration |
| Deployment accounting | MIT notice obligations | Terms can vary by developer, location, project, SaaS/OEM, air gap, and support coverage |
| PowerShell | First-party PSWriteOffice | Application-specific wrapper required |

## Choose Iron Software when

- a specific Iron engine wins the representative-file evaluation;
- commercial support and its purchasing model fit the organization;
- the team accepts license-key configuration and the required deployment coverage;
- source modification and an MIT redistribution model are not requirements.

## Choose OfficeIMO when

- the application requires MIT licensing, inspectable source, or local patching;
- one workflow spans Office, PDF, Outlook messages and stores, OneNote, open formats, and RAG-oriented extraction;
- PowerShell users need a maintained first-party surface;
- format operations and conversion losses should be documented and verified without a commercial license key.

## Price is not the only deployment variable

Do not compare a single advertised price with “free” and stop. Record developers, build agents, locations, applications, SaaS or OEM redistribution, air-gapped development, updates, support, and future team growth. On the technical side, test rendering, fonts, charts, formulas, forms, signatures, attachments, malformed files, and readback with the exact version that will ship.
