Planning that survives contact

Tip. Drag a task onto a teammate's avatar to reassign it. Lantern moves the dependent tasks and shows the new end date before you confirm.

Plans change. Lantern treats a plan as a living thing: when a date moves, everything that depends on it moves too, and the people affected get one short summary instead of a pile of notifications.

Each task carries its context with it: the conversation, the files, the decisions and who made them. New joiners can read the history of a project in minutes rather than asking around. Templates capture how your team likes to run recurring work, so the second campaign takes half the effort of the first.

Built for developers too

Lantern has a small, predictable API. Tokens are scoped per workspace, every call is logged, and webhooks retry with backoff for 24 hours.

curl https://api.example.com/v2/tasks \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -d '{"title":"Publish October report","due":"2026-11-14","owner":"maya"}'

A response returns the task with its identifier and resolved dates:

{
  "id": "tsk_8f21c",
  "title": "Publish October report",
  "due": "2026-11-14",
  "status": "scheduled"
}
